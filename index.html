Why Fields? Spacetime & Symmetry

Relativity plus quantum uncertainty forces multi-particle creation, demotes position x to a spacetime label, and classifies all possible particles by Poincaré group representations.

Crisis of Relativistic QM & Birth of the Quantum Field

Two measurements separated faster than light cannot influence one another because the particle propagation from y → x and antiparticle propagation from x → y cancel identically.

Action Principle, Euler–Lagrange & Noether’s Theorem

When a continuous transformation δφ_a changes L only by a four-divergence ∂_μ J^μ, the local flow j^μ satisfies ∂_μ j^μ = 0 on classical trajectories, locking total charge Q = ∫ d³x j⁰ constant in time.

Poincaré Group, Wigner’s Little Group & Spin Classification

Complexifying rotations J_i and boosts K_i splits the Lorentz algebra into two decoupled SU(2) spins (j_L, j_R), classifying every field representation in nature.

Every Fourier mode k becomes a quantum harmonic oscillator; the +iε contour keeps positive energy moving forward in time; functional derivatives of Z[J] tame combinatorics.

Stop 04
Canonical Quantization of Scalar Fields & Zero-Point Vacuum Energy

Promoting field φ and conjugate momentum π = φ̇ to operators turns Fourier amplitudes into ladder operators a_p, a_p† that add or remove quanta of energy ω_p.

Stop 05
The Feynman Propagator & Complex p⁰ Pole Prescription (+iε)

Time-ordering T puts whichever field occurs later on the left, and the infinitesimal iε nudges poles at p⁰ = ±(ω_p − iε) so closing the contour picks up forward-time quanta.

Stop 06
Feynman Path Integral & Generating Functional Z[J]

Coupling a probe source J(x) turns the infinite-dimensional path integral of a free field into a single Gaussian exponential of source pairs linked by the Feynman propagator D_F(x−y).

Stop 07
Interacting Fields, Dyson’s Formula, Wick’s Theorem & Symmetry Factors

Expectation values in the unknown interacting vacuum |Ω⟩ reduce to free-field contractions weighted by exp(iS_int), with disconnected vacuum bubbles canceling between numerator and denominator.

Stop 08
The LSZ Reduction Formula: Amputating Legs to Reach the S-Matrix

Multiplying the Fourier-transformed n-point Green’s function by inverse propagators (p_k² − m²)/i strips off external legs and isolates the exact on-shell scattering matrix element iM.

Stop 09
From iM to Cross Sections dσ/dΩ, Decay Widths Γ & the Optical Theorem

Differential cross section equals kinematic flux & phase-space ratio times |M|²; unitarity S†S = 1 forces the imaginary part of forward loop scattering to equal the total cross section!

3 · Ten Popular Myths & Graduate Calculation Sign Traps

Every grad student loses hours to at least three of these traps—bookmark them before doing loop homework.

Trap #01 · Conceptual Ontology

“Virtual particles temporarily violate energy conservation ΔE·Δt ~ ħ during scattering.”

✓ QFT Reality: 4-momentum p^μ = (E, p⃗) is STRICTLY conserved at every Feynman vertex via (2π)⁴δ⁽⁴⁾(∑p)!

What differs from a free particle is the invariant mass-shell relation: an internal propagator carries off-shell 4-momentum p² = E² − |p⃗|² ≠ m² (e.g. t-channel photon exchange has q² < 0 spacelike!). ΔE·Δt comes from old non-covariant time-ordered perturbation theory, which covariant Feynman diagrams replaced in 1949.

Trap #02 · Sign & Convention Trap

“Mixing formulas from Peskin/Schwartz and Srednicki works without checking the metric signature.”

✓ QFT Reality: West Coast (+−−−) gives p² = +m² and +½(∂φ)²; East Coast (−+++) gives p² = −m² and −½(∂φ)²!

Every single Lorentz contraction k·p, propagator i/(p²−m²) vs −i/(p²+m²), Clifford {γ^μ,γ^ν} = ±2η^{μν}, and spin sum ∑uū = ±p̸ + m flips! Use the Metric Switch at the top of this site whenever cross-reading Srednicki/Weinberg alongside Tong/Schwartz/Peskin.

Trap #03 · Fermion Sign Trap

“Two Feynman diagrams for the same process always add with a + sign: M = M_1 + M_2.”

✓ QFT Reality: Swapping two identical external fermion lines OR adding a closed fermion/ghost loop introduces a mandatory (−1)!

In Bhabha (e⁺e⁻→e⁺e⁻) or Møller (e⁻e⁻→e⁻e⁻) scattering, the s/t or t/u diagrams differ by exchanging two anticommuting fermion operators, forcing M = M_1 − M_2! Getting this sign wrong destroys destructive Pauli interference at θ = 90°.

Trap #04 · Spinor Chirality Trap

“Chirality (left/right-handedness) and helicity (spin along momentum) are the same thing.”

✓ QFT Reality: Chirality (γ⁵ = ±1) is Lorentz-invariant; helicity (S⃗·p̂) flips if you boost faster than a massive particle!

Only for strictly m = 0 particles traveling at c do chirality and helicity coincide. That is why charged pion decay π⁻ → e⁻ ν̄_e is helicity-suppressed by (m_e/m_μ)² ≈ 2.3 × 10⁻⁵ compared to π⁻ → μ⁻ ν̄_μ: the spin-0 pion forces the left-chiral electron into the ‘wrong’ positive-helicity state, which is only possible via its tiny mass m_e!

Trap #05 · Gauge Calculation Trap

“You can always replace external spin-1 polarization sums ∑_λ ε_μ ε_ν* by −η_{μν} in QCD just like QED.”

✓ QFT Reality: TRUE for 1 external gluon or Abelian QED—FALSE for ≥ 2 external gluons in non-Abelian QCD!

In QED, Ward’s identity k^μ M_μ = 0 holds even when other photons are off-shell. In Yang–Mills QCD, k_1^μ M_{μν} vanishes ONLY when the second gluon is contracted with a physical transverse polarization ε_2^ν! Using −η_{μν} on both external gluons produces fake unphysical polarizations unless you subtract external Faddeev–Popov ghost pair production!

Trap #06 · Renormalization Myth

“Non-renormalizable theories (with d > 4 operators like gravity G_N or Fermi G_F) have zero predictive power.”

✓ QFT Reality: Non-renormalizable Effective Field Theories are 100% predictive at energies E ≪ Λ to any desired accuracy (E/Λ)^N!

To compute scattering at precision (E/Λ)², you only need FINITELY many Wilson coefficients up to dimension d = 6! General Relativity quantized as an EFT predicts unambiguous quantum gravitational loop corrections to Newton’s potential V(r) = −(Gm_1m_2/r)[1 + 41 Għ/(10π r²c³)]!

Trap #07 · DimReg Algebra Trap

“In Dimensional Regularization d = 4 − ε, γ_μ γ^ν γ^μ still equals −2γ^ν.”

✓ QFT Reality: η_μ^μ = d = 4 − ε in DimReg, so γ_μ γ^ν γ^μ = (2 − d)γ^ν = −(2 − ε)γ^ν!

Dropping that tiny +εγ^ν when multiplying a 2/ε UV pole misses a finite +2 constant (ε × 2/ε = 2), ruining Ward identities Z_1 = Z_2 and giving the wrong MS-bar finite part!

Trap #08 · Mass Generation Myth

“The Higgs mechanism gives mass to 99% of everyday atomic matter.”

✓ QFT Reality: ~99% of proton and neutron mass (≈ 929 of 938 MeV) comes from massless QCD gluon & quark confinement energy!

Higgs Yukawa couplings give 100% of the electron’s 0.511 MeV mass (setting Bohr atomic radii!) and ~9 MeV to valence u,d quarks. Even in the chiral limit m_u = m_d = 0, QCD dimensional transmutation Λ_QCD generates ~880 MeV nucleon mass!

Trap #09 · Symmetry Breaking Myth

“In the Higgs mechanism, gauge symmetry is broken, so gauge transformations are no longer valid.”

✓ QFT Reality: A local gauge symmetry is a redundancy of description—it can NEVER truly be broken (Elitzur’s Theorem)!

‘Spontaneous gauge symmetry breaking’ is convenient shorthand for the Higgs/Anderson phase where a gauge-invariant composite operator carries non-vanishing stiffness, allowing Goldstone fields to be absorbed by a gauge choice (unitary gauge) into massive spin-1 propagators.

Trap #10 · Anomaly Crisis Trap

“Since DimReg preserves gauge invariance, γ⁵ obeys {γ⁵, γ^μ} = 0 naively in d = 4 − ε dimensions.”

✓ QFT Reality: ε^{μνρσ} and γ⁵ are intrinsically 4-dimensional—naive {γ⁵, γ^μ} = 0 in d ≠ 4 sets the ABJ chiral anomaly to zero by mistake!

In chiral theories you MUST use ’t Hooft–Veltman’s split (γ⁵ anticommutes in 4D subspace, commutes in −ε subspace) or Fujikawa’s path-integral measure regularization to capture triangle anomalies accurately.
