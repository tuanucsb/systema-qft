# Systema Camporum · Graduate Quantum Field Theory & The Standard Model

**Live Interactive Edition:** https://tuanucsb.github.io/systema-qft/

**Systema Camporum** (*System of Fields*) applies the natural-history architecture and step-by-step learning system of *Systema Intelligentiae* to graduate-level Quantum Field Theory and the Standard Model—synthesizing the strongest pedagogical chapters from **David Tong**, **Matthew D. Schwartz**, **Mark Srednicki**, **Peskin & Schroeder**, **Steven Weinberg**, and **Timo Weigand**.

## Highlights

- **5-Floor Architectural Spine (`Overview`)**: Climbs from relativistic lightcone causality up through free spin-0/1/2/1 Fock oscillators, Feynman scattering, Wilsonian renormalization, and non-Abelian gauge theory to the complete Standard Model.
- **24-Stop Graduate Curriculum (`Journey`)**: Seven acts covering a full 2-semester PhD QFT sequence—each stop pairs plain-English physical intuition, merged textbook citations, a decoded master equation, a 4-step graduate proof drawer, an upgrading **8-Part Field Spec-Board**, and a worked checkpoint problem.
- **Interactive Equation Dissector (`Equations`)**: Click every colored chunk across 10 master equations (starting with $\mathcal{L}_{\text{SM}}$) to inspect its plain job, mass dimension `[E]^d`, unpacked Lorentz/spinor/color/weak indices, and qualifying-exam pitfalls.
- **Live Metric Signature Switch (`(+ − − −)` ↔ `(− + + +)`)**: Instantly translates kinetic signs, propagators, and Dirac spin sums between West Coast (Tong, Schwartz, Peskin) and East Coast (Srednicki, Weinberg) conventions.
- **28-Node Dependency Cladogram (`Tree`) & 5 Live Simulators (`5 Labs`)**: Interactive EFT operator power-counting, complex $p^0$-plane $+i\epsilon$ contour & Wick rotation, Feynman diagram-to-amplitude builder, 1-loop SM running couplings $\alpha_i^{-1}(\mu)$, and Mexican-hat Higgs + $N_c = 3$ anomaly auditor.
- **12 Standalone Vector Plates (`plates/*.svg`)**: Light/dark adaptive publication-grade vector diagrams.
